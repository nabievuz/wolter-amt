# RUNBOOK — Insident, ACK va biznes boshqaruvini qaytarish

Holat: spetsifikatsiya · Runtime: NOT_RUN · Sana: 2026-10-23.

Workflow: [WF-O02](../09_Config_Templates/workflows/WF-O02.yaml) (accountable `technical_operator`). Ketma-ketlik: aniqlash → **javobgar ACK** → cheklash → remote/data reconcile → tuzatish → owner gate/resume yoki human handback ACK → kuzatuv → 48 soat ichida AAR. Shubha bo‘lsa avval cheklang: owner kill A ([KILL_SWITCH](KILL_SWITCH.md)); operator owner’ga eskalatsiya qiladi, protective controls esa o‘zi ishlaydi. Kontrakt: [OPERATING_ENVELOPE §5](../11_Production_Contracts/OPERATING_ENVELOPE.md).

## Severity, ACK va eskalatsiya

| Severity | Misol | ACK muddati | Yo‘l |
|---|---|---|---|
| `critical` | nazoratsiz sarf, accepted lead yo‘qolishi, security/PII (jumladan PC yoki disk o‘g‘irlanishi va ruxsatsiz jismoniy kirish belgisi, ◇ P-32), noaniq duplicate/remote effekt, host yo‘qolishi | `ops.ack_minutes.critical` | primary → deputy → owner (alternate channel) |
| `degraded` | data freshness, provider/analytics uzilishi, quota/backup xavfi | `ops.ack_minutes.degraded` | primary → deputy → owner |

Triage (WF-O02 S02) severity’ni tasdiqlaydi yoki `critical` → `degraded` ga tushiradi — faqat katalogda `degraded` bo‘lgan, lekin noma’lum severity sababli `critical` kelgan kod uchun; katalogda `critical` kod (security/PII, host yo‘qolishi, nazoratsiz sarf, intake yo‘qolishi) pasaytirilmaydi, har pasaytirish shaxs bilan yoziladi va owner `/decisions` da ko‘radi. Pasaytirish faqat ACK muddati va eskalatsiyani o‘zgartiradi, containment’ni emas; S02 ACK muddati o‘tganda yopilmaydi (`on_timeout: wait_and_escalate`): deputy yoki owner ACK’i uni yakunlaydi; recovery (S05) tasdiqlangan severity va ACK bergan custodian bilan boshlanadi. Inson handback ACK’i (S09) handback trigger’i (`handback_triggers`: KC-01 `human_handback`, INC-20 zero-qualified exposure, kill B, `protective_pause`, foreign spend) bo‘lsa containment’dan keyin darhol ochiladi — triage ACK’ini ham, recovery’ni ham kutmaydi — va faqat pipeline custodian’ining qabul qilingan `handback_acks` yozuvi bilan yakunlanadi; alertning o‘zi yetmaydi (FR-GOV-10, ✅ Q31). Owner resume step’i (S06) faqat faol himoya nazorati yoki kill latch (`containment_causes`) bo‘lsa, recovery’dan keyin ochiladi. Ikkalasi ham bo‘lmasa (masalan INC-19) `not_applicable` yoziladi va owner buni AAR va `/decisions` da ko‘radi. Severity enum va ACK havolalari — `operations.yaml` `severities`; aniq minutlar va eskalatsiya — `ops.ack_minutes`, `ops.escalation` readiness’ida haqiqiy qamrov bilan. Bu hujjat tayinlanmagan odam yoki minut uydirmaydi. Severity noma’lum bo‘lsa triage’gacha `critical` hisoblanadi. Alert kodlari katalogi — [TELEGRAM §6](../03_Architecture/TELEGRAM.md). ACK = alert’dagi `[Qabul qildim]` tugmasi yoki alternate channel’dagi imzolangan ACK → `incident_acks`; handback qabul qilinishi → `handback_acks`. Telegram yuborilgani yoki o‘qilgani ACK emas. ACK qila oladiganlar — roster’dagi `ops_responder` flag’li odamlar. Owner-only kill/resume/rol/yangi activation saqlanadi. Owner yo‘q bo‘lsa: tasdiqlangan safe state, yangi risk oshishi yo‘q, ad custody’ni faqat Ads Manager vakolati bor odam ACK qiladi.

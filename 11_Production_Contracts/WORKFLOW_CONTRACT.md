# Workflow kontrakti — ishning boshidan natijasigacha kuzatiladigan birligi

Holat: spetsifikatsiya · Runtime: NOT_RUN · Sana: 2026-10-23 · Vaqt: Asia/Tashkent.

Bu hujjat AMT’dagi har biznes, operatsiya va engineering ishining asosiy birligini belgilaydi. **Workflow** — trigger’dan yakuniy natijagacha kuzatiladigan, versiyalangan va durable saqlanadigan ish; agent faqat uning tegishli bosqichini (step) bajaradi. Kontrakt [INVARIANTS](INVARIANTS.md) I16–I18 va I22 ning batafsil shakli. Texnik dizayn: [WORKFLOW_RUNTIME](../03_Architecture/WORKFLOW_RUNTIME.md). Machine sxema: [workflow-definition.schema.json](../09_Config_Templates/schemas/workflow-definition.schema.json), policy: [workflow-policy.yaml](../09_Config_Templates/workflow-policy.yaml), katalog: `09_Config_Templates/workflows/WF-*.yaml`.

Yozilgan kontrakt ishlab turgan runtime dalili emas. Ishlatiladigan mexanizm (AMT’ning o‘z durable ledger’i, Paperclip imkoniyati yoki ularning birikmasi) `workflow.mechanism` readiness’dagi haqiqiy restart/timer/handoff/replay prototipi bilan tanlanadi ([ADR-024](../04_ADR/ADR-024-workflow-runtime-capability-first.md)).

## 1. Qamrov va chegara

| Narsa | Workflow bilan munosabati |
|---|---|
| Command ledger (`change_set_items`, `execution_attempts`) | Alohida ledger bo‘lib qoladi. Tashqi effektli step faqat command orqali; command holati workflow holati bilan almashtirilmaydi |
| Paperclip issue/routine | Agent ishining ko‘rinishi va vazifa yuzasi (projection). Issue yopilishi step’ni tugatmaydi; issue holati workflow truth bilan zid bo‘lsa runtime truth ustun, issue yangilanadi |
| Ops-bot va inson qarorlari | Approval, attestatsiya, ACK — workflow’ga signal; tasdiq vakolati approval-policy va RBAC’da qoladi |
| Gateway va llm-proxy | Tool va model chaqiruvlari har doim guard’lardan o‘tadi; workflow ularni chetlab o‘tmaydi |
| Engineering ishlari | WF-E01…E04 xuddi shu kontraktda; ular biznes agentlariga reklama/CRM vakolati bermaydi |

## 2. Definition kontrakti

Har definition quyidagilarni majburiy belgilaydi (sxemadagi maydon nomlari qavsda):

| Guruh | Talab |
|---|---|
| Identity va maqsad | `workflow_id`, `definition_version`, accountable inson roli (`accountable`), biznes yoki operatsion natija (`purpose`) |
| Boshlash | trigger turi va manbasi, `dedup_key`, `correlation_key`, input schema, precondition va kim boshlay/bekor qila olishi (`authorization`) |
| Bajarish | step/dependency graph; har step uchun agent, deterministik servis yoki inson bajaruvchisi; input/output artifact; handoff qabul/rad sharti; approval policy action; command action va tashqi effekt turi |
| Chegaralar | maksimal davomiylik, step bo‘yicha maksimal urinish, xarajat hisobi (`ai_runtime`, `engineering`, `ads` yoki `none`), concurrency, eskalatsiya va owner yo‘qligidagi xavfsiz holat |
| Davom ettirish | checkpoint, timer, signal/callback, lease/fence, cancel, compensation va noaniq tashqi effektni reconcile qilish usuli |
| Yakunlash | zarur dalillar, texnik yakun, kuzatuv va biznes qabul mezoni (yoki asoslangan `not_applicable`) |
| Xato yo‘llari | yetishmagan input, bekor qilish/eskalatsiya va noaniq tashqi effekt yo‘li — har workflow’da majburiy |
| Testlar | end-to-end va har xato yo‘li uchun test ID (`WF-TEST-*`), tegishli UAT/EVO-AT/F-AT |

Bajaruvchi va principal nomi erkin matn emas — u turiga qarab reyestrdan olinadi (validator W08): `deterministic` → [runtime-isolation.yaml](../09_Config_Templates/runtime-isolation.yaml) `services[].service_id` (systemd UID yoki unit nomi emas: `gateway`, `executor`, `sync-worker`, `ops-bot`, `capi-bridge`) yoki `workflow-policy.yaml#external_participants` dagi `executes_steps: true` yozuv (`landing-intake`, `landing-intake-delivery`, `github-ci`); `agent` → `agents` yoki `engineering_roles`; `human` → roles.yaml roli yoki assignment’i, yoki `workflow-policy.yaml#human_aliases` dagi kompozit nom (`approver_per_policy`, `flag_holder`, `snapshot_provider`, `ops_on_call`, `ops_deputy`). Flag bilan ishlaydigan inson step’i flagni `executor.requires_flag` da nomlaydi (masalan Q42 til tekshiruvi — `flag_holder` + `language_checker`). `handoff.to`, `authorization.may_start`/`may_cancel` va `limits.escalation[].to` ham shu reyestrlardan; eskalatsiya bitta odam roliga boradi (“a and b” ikki yozuvga ajratiladi). `trigger.source_refs` trigger manbasini reyestr id’lari bilan nomlaydi (servis, agent, rol, alias, tashqi ishtirokchi, workflow, `operations.yaml#schedule` id, signal, timer yoki `<config>.yaml#<path>`). Production credential talab qiladigan amal (masalan landing’ni Dokploy’da deploy qilish, Q46.4) deterministik servisga yozilmaydi — u `human` step bo‘ladi, natijasi esa read-only tekshiruv step’i bilan isbotlanadi (WF-B04 S08 → S09). Owner qarorlari yoki ◇ qatorlari tayanadigan bajaruvchilar `workflow-policy.yaml#executor_constraints` da qotirilgan (WF-B04 S08 va WF-O04 S02 — owner, WF-B02 S05 — `language_checker`, kuzatuv step’lari — `sync-worker`, WF-O02 S03 — `amt-control`); qat’iy flag’li alias boshqa `requires_flag` olmaydi va `flag_exclusions` (`language_checker` uchun owner — ◇ P-29 qabul qilinmaguncha) rol va alias’larda ham amal qiladi (validator W08).

Har step natijasi keyingi step’ga kiradi; hech kim iste’mol qilmaydigan step faqat `completion.end_steps` da e’lon qilingan yakun bo‘lishi mumkin (validator W16) — shunday qilib parallel tarmoq jim uzilib qolmaydi (masalan WF-B06 o‘tgan davr tekshiruvi rejaga kiradi).

Inson step’i timeout’i run uchun nimani anglatishi `on_timeout` da yoziladi (validator W20): `wait_and_escalate` — step ochiq qoladi va eskalatsiya oluvchisi uni hali ham yakunlay oladi (masalan WF-O02 S02 ACK), `expire_item_and_close` — tasdiq TTL bilan tugaydi (Q32), `fail_step_safe` yoki `auto_not_applicable` — aytilgan xavfsiz holat bilan. Timeout eskalatsiya chegarasiga teng bo‘lsa `on_timeout` majburiy; eskalatsiya boshqa odamga borsa va step tasdiq bo‘lmasa — faqat `wait_and_escalate`.

Tasdiq bloki bo‘lmagan inson vazifasi uni yakunlaydigan signal(lar)ni `completes_on` da nomlaydi; har biri workflow’ning `continuation.signals` va katalogida bo‘ladi, va kamida bittasining producer’i shu step’ni kutmaydi (validator W21) — masalan WF-B04 S08 deploy vazifasi S09 read-back’i bilan, WF-O04 S02 `[Qo‘ydim]` yoki mos read-back bilan yopiladi.

Shartli step `when: {step, output, in | non_empty}` bilan yoziladi: shart yuqoridagi (transitive `depends_on`) step’ning nomlangan natijasiga qaraydi — `cause` (o‘sha step’ning `approval.by_cause` sabablari), `severity` (`operations.yaml#severities`) yoki o‘sha step `output_fields` ro‘yxatida e’lon qilgan tuzilgan maydon — `outputs` matnidagi so‘z emas (validator W15). ◇ qatorlar tayangan shartlar [workflow-policy.yaml](../09_Config_Templates/workflow-policy.yaml) `required_conditions` da qadalgan (WF-O02 S06 S10ning fresh `remaining_owner_causes`i, S09 `handback_triggers`, WF-B04 S05 `cause: offer_change`, WF-B02 S07 `created_entity_ids`); `executor_constraints` da `unconditional: true` deb belgilangan himoya step’i (WF-O02 S03 containment) `when` olmaydi. Shart bajarilmasa step `skipped` (`condition_false`) deb yoziladi, qayta urinilmaydi va undan keyingi step’lar davom etadi.

Biznes qiymati hali tasdiqlanmagan chegara (masalan, maturity horizon) uydirilmaydi: `readiness:<key>` bilan bog‘lanadi va o‘sha kalit tasdiqlanmaguncha live profil yopiq qoladi. Definition o‘zgarishi yangi `definition_version` va digest beradi; boshlangan run eski definition’ga pin bo‘lib qoladi yoki WF-E02 orqali sinalgan state migration bilan ko‘chiriladi. Eski run yangi definition’ni jim yuklamaydi.

## 3. Run yozuvi

Har run quyidagilarni saqlaydi: `run_id`, `parent_run_id`, `root_run_id`, `workflow_id`, `definition_version`, `definition_digest`, `release_id`, biznes entity reference’lari (pipeline, submission, brief, proposal va hokazo), exact artifact/model/prompt/skill/config/policy revision’lari, joriy step, kutilayotgan input yoki approval, faol timerlar, `controller_epoch`, holat, biznes natija va audit zanjiri.

| Holat turi | Qiymatlar | Kim yozadi |
|---|---|---|
| Run | `created`, `running`, `waiting`, `blocked`, `reconciling`, `completed`, `failed`, `cancelled`, `manual_resolution` | faqat `amt-workflow` |
| Step | `pending`, `ready`, `claimed`, `running`, `waiting`, `handoff_pending`, `completed`, `rejected`, `failed`, `skipped`, `cancelled`, `compensated` | `amt-workflow` (bajaruvchi natija signalini beradi) |
| Handoff | `offered`, `accepted`, `rejected`, `expired` | qabul qiluvchi tekshiruvidan keyin `amt-workflow` |
| Biznes natija | `pending`, `met`, `not_met`, `inconclusive`, `not_applicable` | tegishli o‘lchov step’i, owner qarori bilan |
| Command | INVARIANTS I05 dagi kanonik enum | gateway/executor/ops — o‘zgarmaydi |
| Vendor holati | provider’dan o‘qilgan haqiqiy remote state | reconciliation |

Run, step va handoff o‘tishlari (kim, guard, yakuniy va taqiqlangan o‘tishlar) — [workflow-policy.yaml](../09_Config_Templates/workflow-policy.yaml) `state_machines`; command, change-set item, Trust Ramp, kill criteria seriyasi va kill rejimlari bilan birga [STATE_MACHINES.md](../03_Architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) da (validator W12, ◇ P-166). To‘rt holat bir-birini almashtirmaydi. Command-linked step `workflow-policy.yaml#command_aggregation` dagi roli, `complete_when` va birinchi mos tartibli qoida bilan tugaydi; dispatch `uncertain` bandni reconciliation’ga o‘tkazib `completed` bo‘lishi mumkin. Bu command muvaffaqiyati emas: `succeeded`/`done_manual` faqat remote evidence bilan. Run-owned unresolved band run’ni ochiq saqlaydi. `manual_resolution_handoff` qoidasi bo‘yicha faqat `manual_resolution` bandini durable owner task **va I18 accepted handoff** bilan run custody’sidan chiqarish mumkin; exact revision/attempt/evidence, receiver/time va reservation/conflict-lock reference’lari saqlanadi, reservation/lock bo‘shamaydi, retry yoki success yozilmaydi. Task yoki alertning o‘zi qabul emas. Terminal dalil yoki accepted custody transfer’dan keyin avval `running` checkpoint’i, keyin odatiy completion; `reconciling`/`manual_resolution`dan bevosita `completed` taqiqlangan. Command’siz step o‘z output/approval/`completes_on` dalili bilan tugaydi. Paperclip issue, agent matni yoki suhbatdagi “ha” dalil o‘rnini bosmaydi.

## 4. Durable davom ettirish

- **Checkpoint:** har step o‘tishi audit va notification outbox bilan bitta tranzaksiyada yoziladi. Restart oxirgi checkpoint’dan davom etadi; tugagan step qayta bajarilmaydi.
- **Timer:** DB’dagi durable yozuv; process `sleep`i yoki ochiq LLM sessiyasi emas. Ko‘p kunlik kutish hech qanday process yoki modelni ushlab turmaydi.
- **Signal va callback:** `correlation_key` + signal ID bo‘yicha dedup. Kechikkan callback ochiq run’ga biriktiriladi, yopilgan run uchun faqat auditga yoziladi va tashqi harakat chiqarmaydi. Har iste’mol qilinadigan signal [workflow-policy.yaml](../09_Config_Templates/workflow-policy.yaml) `signals.catalog`da producer (registry servisi, uni `emits`da yozgan step yoki `external_participants`), transport, payload va dedup kaliti bilan; har timer `timer_catalog`da `operations.yaml#schedule` id’si, readiness kaliti, input maydoni yoki nisbiy asos bilan (validator W10). Producer’siz signal yoki iste’molchisiz producer — spetsifikatsiya xatosi.
- **Lease/fence:** step claim lease va monoton fence bilan; lease olib qo‘yilganda eski worker natijasi qabul qilinmaydi.
- **Controller epoch:** workflow controller’i epoch bilan ishlaydi. Eski epoch’dagi dispatch rad etiladi va auditga yoziladi (I22).
- **Retry:** faqat definition’da `safe_resume` yoki `new_attempt_after_reconcile` deb belgilangan step. Qaytarib bo‘lmaydigan tashqi effekt avval reconcile qilinadi; provider exactly-once kafolati taxmin qilinmaydi.
- **Cancel va compensation:** bekor qilish pending command’larni `cancelled`/`expired` qiladi; jo‘natilgan effektni bekor qilish yangi, vakolati tekshirilgan command (`compensates_command_id`).
- **Concurrency:** `limits.concurrency` [workflow-policy.yaml](../09_Config_Templates/workflow-policy.yaml) `concurrency_semantics` bo‘yicha o‘qiladi: barcha ochiq run’lar (`created`, `running`, `waiting`, `blocked`, `reconciling`, `manual_resolution`) sanaladi; trigger avval `dedup_key` bo‘yicha ochiq run’ga biriktiriladi, keyin limit tekshiriladi; limit to‘lsa definition’dagi `on_limit` (`queue`, `skip_with_audit`) yoki standart `reject_with_alert` (ALERT_WORKFLOW_BLOCKED) qo‘llanadi. Trigger davridan uzun kuzatuv jadvalli run’ning oxirgi step’i bo‘lmaydi: u kuzatilayotgan obyekt bo‘yicha alohida workflow’da ishlaydi — command ta’siri WF-B07’da (validator W09).

## 5. Ijro zanjiri va dalil

Har tashqi natija quyidagi zanjir bilan tekshiriladi: **workflow run → step → artifact revision → approval → command revision → execution attempt → remote evidence**. Har bo‘g‘in oldingisiga ID bilan bog‘lanadi. Zanjirning biror bo‘g‘ini yo‘q bo‘lsa natija “bajarildi” deb ko‘rsatilmaydi; hisobot “bajarildi/live” iborasini faqat shu zanjir to‘liq bo‘lganda ishlatadi (FR-RPT-02).

Tasdiq va dispatch bir-biridan ajratiladi (validator W11): `approval` faqat `human` step’da turadi; `command_actions` faqat `executor` servisining deterministik step’ida va faqat shu workflow’dagi yuqoridagi (transitive `depends_on`) inson tasdiq step’i o‘sha action’ni nomlagandan keyin. Har command action holati bitta workflow’ga tegishli (`workflow-policy.yaml#dispatch_ownership`): yaratish (`asset_upload`, `ad_create`, `campaign_create`, `adset_duplicate`) va birinchi aktivatsiya (`campaign_activate`, yangi ad uchun `creative_activate`) — WF-B02; budget, pauza va avval faol bo‘lgan entity’ni qayta yoqish (`entity_activate`, `creative_activate` `ever_active` bo‘lsa) — WF-B03. Gateway holati dispatch qilayotgan workflow’ga mos kelmaydigan command’ni rad etadi. Boshqa workflow’ga tegishli action kerak bo‘lsa (masalan WF-O02 da `protective_pause` dan keyingi qayta yoqish) sabab `by_cause.approved_in: WF-B03` va `approved_case` bilan yoziladi va qaror egasining tasdiq navbatida, aynan shu nomli holatda olinadi — bir action ikki joyda tasdiqlanmaydi va ikki joydan dispatch qilinmaydi. Holat bo‘yicha bo‘lingan action’ni dispatch qiluvchi step o‘zi xizmat qiladigan holatni `serves_cases` da nomlaydi; oddiy `policy_action` boshqa workflow’ga tegishli command action’ni tasdiqlay olmaydi; tasdiq step’ining bajaruvchisi u sanagan har holatni tasdiqlay oladigan rolga ega bo‘ladi (owner-only action uchun owner). Qarorni oladigan holat manba holatidan yumshoq bo‘lmaydi: manba `never_auto` bo‘lsa u ham `never_auto`, tasdiqlovchilari manbanikidan keng emas (W11). `protective_pause` dan keyingi qayta yoqish WF-B03 S04 `re_activation_after_protective_pause` holatida faqat owner va hech qachon avtomatik emas (ARCHITECTURE §11.3): ads_approver ham, L2 policy approval ham uni tasdiqlay olmaydi. `by_cause.approvers` holat uchun policy tasdiqlovchilarini faqat toraytiradi.

Xarajat: agent step’i bor har definition o‘z run cap’ini nomlaydi (`limits.cost.max_minor` yoki `max_minor_ref` — `budget.request_bounds` `run_caps_minor`, engineering’da `engineering.budget` `per_proposal_max_minor`) va LLM/media so‘rovlarini `ai_runtime` yoki `engineering` hisobiga yozadi ([workflow-policy.yaml](../09_Config_Templates/workflow-policy.yaml) `cost_caps`, validator M09). Har so‘rov `run_id` ga yoziladi; cap’dan oshiradigan so‘rov `run_cost_cap` bilan rad etiladi, step `blocked`, `ALERT_WORKFLOW_BLOCKED`; deterministik himoya ishi (pauza, kill, reconciliation) hech qachon cap bilan to‘xtamaydi. Bu cap F1_COST_RUNTIME dan boshlab loop breaker: F4 smoke, F5 eval va shadow ham uning ostida ishlaydi. Reklama sarfi run cap’iga yozilmaydi — u har command’da `budgets.yaml` ads va eksperimentda `business.exposure` bilan cheklanadi.

Parent/child: child run’ni faqat parent step’ning `starts` ro‘yxati boshlaydi — `{workflow, key, input, input_values, await, on_parent_end, condition}`; `amt-workflow` uni parent run nomidan `parent_run_id`, `root_run_id` va parent step bilan yaratadi. Har start [workflow-policy.yaml](../09_Config_Templates/workflow-policy.yaml) `pinned_starts` da qayd etilgan va o‘zini tasdiqlaydigan inson step’idan pastda turadi (WF-B06 S06, WF-B05 S03, WF-B04 S04, WF-E01 S03/S10); child `trigger.kinds` da `parent_run`, `parent_workflows` da aynan uni boshlaydigan workflow’lar, `may_start` `pinned_may_start` ga teng va `child_guard` sharti bilan; parent child’ning barcha `required_fields` ini beradi. Agent step’i run boshlamaydi: WF-B02 S08 «iterate» — CMO’ga so‘rov, yangi WF-B02 ni CMO, owner yoki ads_approver boshlaydi. `await: none` parent’ni kutdirmaydi, `started` — child yaratilganda step tugaydi, rad etilgan start step’ni blocker bilan to‘xtatadi (hech qachon «bajarildi» emas), `child_completed` — `child_run_completed` signaligacha kutadi. WF-E01 S07 → WF-E02 `started`: class gate butun handover’ni emas, WF-E02 S03 dagi `handover_plan_approved` signalini kutadi. Eksperiment round’i `round = experiment:<experiment_id>` bilan — bir sanada ikki eksperiment bitta run’ga tushmaydi. Parent cancel, failure yoki kill bo‘lsa `child_lifecycle`: `on_parent_end: cancel_open` child’ni tashqi ta’siri bo‘lmaguncha bekor qiladi (`amt-workflow` shu child’larning `may_cancel` ida), `continue` uni o‘z qoidalarida qoldiradi; WF-B05 to‘xtashi arm revert’larini urgent item sifatida yuboradi va `experiment_lock` ni revert’lar remote dalil bilan terminal bo‘lguncha ushlaydi; S04 dan o‘tgan WF-E02 o‘z rollback’i bilan yuradi; har biznes parent va child `kill` ni iste’mol qiladi; start grafi siklsiz. WF-O02 AAR’idagi saboq WF-E04 ni to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri boshlamaydi — `evo.signal.submit` bilan WF-E01 ga boradi (validator W13, WF-TEST-RT-CHILD-START).

## 6. Handoff kontrakti

Handoff [handoff.schema.json](../09_Config_Templates/schemas/handoff.schema.json) konvertida yuboriladi. 1–2-bandlar (sxema, `analysis` konverti — [analysis-envelope.schema.json](../09_Config_Templates/schemas/analysis-envelope.schema.json), run pin’i, `as_of` freshness, `round` chegarasi, payload sxemasi, guard 4 matn va PII) `workflow.submit_output` da gateway tomonidan deterministik tekshiriladi ([workflow-policy.yaml](../09_Config_Templates/workflow-policy.yaml) `handoff_validation`, validator W19); o‘tmasa producer’ga `invalid_args` qaytadi va handoff qabul qiluvchiga umuman ko‘rsatilmaydi. Qabul qiluvchi faqat ma’noli bandlarni (3–4, `accept_when`) baholaydi:

1. Sxema, `workflow_id`, `definition_digest` va `release_id` mos.
2. Input’lar yangi: offer/asset/review/policy revision’lari hali amalda, `as_of` freshness chegarasida.
3. Qabul qiluvchida shu step uchun vakolat bor; topshiriq uning rolidan tashqariga chiqmaydi.
4. `done_when` va kerakli input bir ma’noli; PII yo‘q; raqamlar manba va vaqt bilan.

Natija `accepted` yoki sabab kodi bilan `rejected`; muddat o‘tsa `expired`. Har handoff definition’da rad etilganda nima bo‘lishini `on_reject` bilan e’lon qiladi ([workflow-policy.yaml](../09_Config_Templates/workflow-policy.yaml) `handoff_rejection`, validator W17): `producer_retry` — producer step (o‘zi yoki yuqoridagi step) rad kodlari bilan yangi revision tayyorlaydi, ko‘pi bilan `max_rounds` marta; bir producer’ni nomlagan barcha handoff’lar uning `max_attempts_per_step` chegarasini bo‘lishadi (1 + `max_rounds` ≤ `max_attempts_per_step`), konvertdagi `round` har qayta topshirishda oshadi; `escalate` — run `blocked` bo‘ladi va blocker `escalate_to` ga (`workflow.report_blocker`, ops-bot) boradi; `escalate_to` harakat qila oladigan principal — definition’ning `may_cancel` dagi odami yoki `may_start` dagi rol/agent (o‘rniga yangi run), hech qachon producer yoki receiver agent emas; avtomatik yangi round bo‘lmaydi, run cancel yoki step timeout bilan chiqadi (`timeout_exit`: `escalation_timeout` sababi bilan `failed`); `end_run` — run `handoff_rejected:<kod>` sababi bilan `failed` tugaydi, keyingi step boshlanmaydi; faqat run’da oldin tashqi ta’sir (reversible/irreversible) yoki child start bo‘lmagan bo‘lsa; `compensate_then_end` — tashqi ta’siri yoki jonli child arm’i bor run avval `continuation.compensation` ni bajaradi (har jonli ta’sir pauza yoki kamaytirish bilan, `dispatch_ownership.urgent_entry` orqali), barcha compensation command’lari remote dalil bilan terminal bo‘lguncha lock’larini (`experiment_lock`) ushlab turadi, keyin `failed` tugaydi. `after_max` — round’lar yoki step urinishlari (texnik retry va `invalid_args` tuzatishlari bilan umumiy) qaysi biri oldin tugasa. Har handoff uchun ko‘pi bilan `max_rounds_cap` = 2 qayta topshirish. `overrides` kodlarni darhol boshqa yo‘lga yuboradi (masalan `offer_changed`, `needs_owner`; `stale_input` — input snapshot step’i qayta ishlaydi); xavfsizlik kodlari (`unauthorized_action`, `protected_touch_without_class`, `authority_change_undeclared`) run’ni tugatadi va owner’ga blocker xabari boradi (`notify: owner`). Har handoff’ning yo‘li `pinned_handoff_routes` da qayd etilgan va validator uni aynan solishtiradi. Shu bilan Creative Studio ↔ Reviewer (WF-B02 S03/S04: Reviewer verdict’ini gateway deterministik tekshiradi, 2 round, keyin CMO), Analyst ↔ Reviewer (WF-B06 S02/S03: 2 round, keyin owner’ga «hisobot kechikdi»), Media Buyer ↔ gateway (WF-B03 S02: 2 round, keyin round tugaydi) va eksperiment verdict’i (WF-B05 S06: analyst 2 round qayta beradi; `exposure_exceeded` — `compensate_then_end`) sikllari chegaralangan. Paperclip assignment, matndagi mention yoki issue status qabul o‘rnini bosmaydi. Eskirgan artifact publish qilinmaydi (EVO-AT13).

## 7. Majburiy xato yo‘llari

Har definition kamida to‘rt yo‘lni yozadi va testlaydi: asosiy muvaffaqiyat; yetishmagan yoki eskirgan input (`blocked` + ega + muddat); bekor qilish yoki eskalatsiya, jumladan owner yo‘qligi (kutadi yoki oldindan tasdiqlangan xavfsiz holat); noaniq tashqi effekt (`uncertain` → reconcile → `succeeded`/`failed_confirmed`/`manual_resolution`). Natijasi noaniq ish “muvaffaqiyatli” yoki “bajarilmadi” deb taxmin qilinmaydi.

## 9. Owner ko‘rinishi

Owner uchun portfel va har run bo‘yicha besh savolga javob ko‘rinadi: **nima bajarilmoqda; qanday natija kutilmoqda; kimdan nima kerak; qancha sarflandi; keyingi qadam nima**. Qo‘shimcha ravishda blocker, kutish muddati (`waiting_until`), qaror deadline’i va oxirgi ishonchli natija (manba va vaqti bilan) ko‘rsatiladi. Ko‘rinish ops-bot (`/status`, `/work`, `/decisions`) va Paperclip dashboard’da (`workflow-policy.yaml#owner_view`); ikkalasi ham `amt-workflow` truth’idan o‘qiydi. Ichki log yoki source yo‘li kerakli biznes qarorini almashtirmaydi; javobsiz qaror deadline’dan oldin eslatiladi va muddat o‘tsa definition’dagi xavfsiz holat qo‘llanadi.

## 10. Boshlang‘ich holat va keyingi o‘tishlar

Tizim noldan quriladi; workflow runtime birinchi biznes dispatch’dan oldin ishlaydi, shuning uchun ishlab turgan oldingi run, command yoki issue’ni ko‘chirish talab qilinmaydi. Qurilish davomida (F1–F4) test sifatida yaratilgan Paperclip issue’lar va sintetik run’lar F5 shadow’dan oldin inventarizatsiya qilinadi: yopiladi yoki tegishli run’ga biriktiriladi; hech biri yashirin holda qolmaydi. Keyingi har runtime, orkestratsiya yoki definition o‘zgarishi WF-E02 handover protokoli bilan bajariladi: takroriy dispatch cheklanadi, tashqi effekti noaniq ish `reconciling`/`manual_resolution` bo‘ladi, provider exactly-once kafolati taxmin qilinmaydi.

## 11. Logical yozuvlar, grant va retention

`workflow_runs`, `step_runs`, `workflow_events`, `handoffs`, `artifact_refs`, `workflow_timers`, `workflow_signals` va `controller_epochs` yozuvlarining maydonlari, grantlari va retention’i [WORKFLOW_RUNTIME §3](../03_Architecture/WORKFLOW_RUNTIME.md) da. Asosiy shartlar: yozuvchi faqat `amt_workflow` DB roli; agentlar faqat sanitizatsiyalangan view orqali o‘qiydi; PII saqlanmaydi (custody reference yetarli); audit append-only; retention `privacy.retention`, `recovery.full_datasets` va `workflow.recovery` bilan tasdiqlanadi. Konkret storage implementatsiyasi ADR-024 dagi capability test natijasiga mos tanlanadi.

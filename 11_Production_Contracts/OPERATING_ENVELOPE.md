## 2. Data freshness va capacity

Tracking bir kun fail — alert; ikki ketma-ket kun fail — global `write_mode=read_only` (✅ Q37). Missing yoki stale health/job — alohida freshness guard: risk oshiruvchi yangi dispatch blok. Kunlik natija bo‘lmagani avtomatik healthy emas. Check holatlari va streak qoidasi [CONFIG_CONTRACT](CONFIG_CONTRACT.md) §5 da (`warn` ta’siri ⧗ OD-17). `read_only`dan chiqish — `tracking.thresholds` da tasdiqlangan N ketma-ket PASS kuni va owner `/resume` (◇ P-18). Freshness TTL va monitor cutoff’lari `tracking.freshness`, `fleet.freshness` va `sale.readiness` da tasdiqlanadi. CAPI lag p95 maqsadi (15 daqiqa, gate emas) normal yuklama konvertida o‘lchanadi; konvert — `ops.resource_envelope` (F5_GO, real shadow’da o‘lchanadi).

Rent: bugungi valid fizik snapshot bo‘lmasa **capacity hold** darhol — odatiy rent budget o‘zgarmaydi. Ritm `operations.yaml#schedule` dan: 08:00 so‘rov, 09:00 eslatma, rent takliflari `fleet_snapshot_accepted` hodisasida, 09:45 cutoff, 10:00 change set, 14:00 eskalatsiya va kun bo‘yi hold (◇ P-06, ⧗ OD-14; [BUSINESS_CONTRACT](BUSINESS_CONTRACT.md) §5.1). Eslatma va eskalatsiya eski ma’lumot bilan ishlashga ruxsat emas. Serviceable = rented + reserved + available; total = serviceable + maintenance. Utilization rented/serviceable; denominator nol — `unknown`. Waitlist bandlari `waitlist_eligible_ready` dan (vaqtinchalik qoida, ⧗ OD-07); threshold, hysteresis va floor — `fleet.thresholds`, waitlist qoidalari — `fleet.waitlist`.

Sale increase uchun stock, fulfilment, financing va backlog readiness valid bo‘lishi kerak; rent’dan sale’ga pul ko‘chirish ham shu gate’dan o‘tadi (`sale.readiness`, gate F6_STEP2, ◇ P-09). Tasdiqlangan business exposure’dan tashqari demand yaratilmaydi.

## 3. Biznes acceptance va exposure

Qualified — North Star; contract/paid/fulfilment/cancel/refund va realized cash/contribution — guardrail. `business.acceptance` har pipeline uchun target, comparable baseline, qualification/tracking revision’lari, minimal sample va maturity oynasini belgilaydi. `business.baseline`, `business.attribution`, `business.outcome_sources`, `business.contribution_policy`, `sales.sop` va `sales.qa` source va counting semantikasini yopadi. 14 kunning o‘zi yetilgan kogorta emas. Har metrika `data_status` bilan chiqadi ([BUSINESS_CONTRACT](BUSINESS_CONTRACT.md) §8.2).

Spend>0, qualified=0 holatida qualified CPL nol emas — aniqlanmagan (`spend_zero_qualified`). `business.exposure` maksimal spend, vaqt va yomon hafta seriyasini, signal, scale blok va inson handback yo‘lini tasdiqlaydi. Past N, yetilmagan kogorta yoki attribution yetishmasligi — `inconclusive`: scale blok va kelishilgan cheklangan exposure; jim PASS emas. Owner hold, uzaytirish yoki handback qarorini haqiqiy manba bilan yozadi. F6 Step 2 va F7 qabuliga shu mezonlar tegishli.

Trust Ramp faqat deterministik hisoblangan (WF-B07 S02), sanaladigan `met` natijali jonli toza item’larni sanaydi; boshqa har natija juftlik seriyasini 0 ga qaytaradi (◇ P-42). Shadow, `inconclusive` (◇ P-04), salbiy yoki audit/revision kamchiligi bor natija graduation’ga kirmaydi. Graduation: o‘sha action×pipeline’da 10 ketma-ket `met` item va oxirgi hisoblangan item’dan keyin 14 kun incident’siz (★), so‘ng owner `trust_graduation` (HG-L2-01). NEVER_AUTO ro‘yxati o‘zgarmaydi.

## 6. Recovery shartnomasi

RPO — ma’lumot yo‘qotish oynasi: eng yangi yaroqli lokal snapshot’ning consistency point’idan mantiqiy xatogacha (`operations.yaml#rpo_definition`). Mantiqiy xato uchun umumiy RPO ≤24 soat (★) saqlanadi; bu har biznes klassiga yetarli degani emas. AMT host yo‘qolishi — Q55 doirasi: AMT tarixi uchun RPO berilmaydi, chunki u yo‘qoladi. `recovery.intake_datasets` va `recovery.full_datasets` har class/host uchun source of truth, backup consistency, shifrlash va kalit custody, retention, tasdiqlangan RPO/RTO (sekund), restore tartibi va o‘lchangan natijani saqlaydi ([CONFIG_CONTRACT](CONFIG_CONTRACT.md) §4.1). RTO kelishilmaguncha readiness `pending`. Process yo‘qolishi va butun host yo‘qolishi alohida baholanadi. AMT PC apparati ishdan chiqsa disklar boshqa PC’ga ko‘chiriladi (shifrlanmagan, Q54); ikkala disk yo‘qolsa yangi PC `00-owner-bootstrap.sh --rebuild` bilan quriladi, git secret’lari age kalitining qog‘oz/QR nusxasi bilan ochiladi (Q56). Host RTO provisioning, kalit, tunnel va Tailscale ulanishi, restore yoki manbalardan qayta qurish, reconcile va validatsiyani o‘z ichiga oladi; zaxira PC yo‘qligi uchun u qismlar yig‘indisi sifatida baholanadi, har qism `measured` yoki `estimated` (F0 o‘rnatish va bootstrap vaqti, `--rebuild` farqi, kalit va tunnel qadamlari, `restore_drill` dagi manbalardan qayta qurish), to‘liq mashq sifatida emas (Q55). Har klass uchun alohida qaror: landing qabul qilgan murojaat (process restart’da yo‘qotish yo‘q — durable commit; host yo‘qolishi uchun alohida durability qarori), approval/audit/kill holati/write ledger, qayta yig‘iladigan warehouse snapshot’lari, Langfuse komponentlari, config/kod/manifest va inson handback’i (data RPO emas, javob muddati).

Majburiy qamrov ikki joyda. **AMT PC’dagi lokal snapshot** (Q55; har kecha 02:00 va har upgrade oldidan, shu RAID1 da, 7 kun, shifrlanmagan — Q54; har artefakt sha256 bilan `MANIFEST`): tarkibi va olish tartibi bitta ro‘yxatda — `operations.yaml#local_snapshot.contents` va `order`. **Production Dokploy’dagi owner backup’i** (HG-F2-18): landing durable custody/outbox/kalitlar va production Umami DB/upload/config. Git secret’larini ochadigan age kalitining qog‘oz/QR nusxasi seyfda (Q56). Git repo DB, object store yoki kalit nusxasini almashtirmaydi. Vendor CRM/Meta asl manba bo‘lib qoladi, lekin AMT custody, control va consent ledger’i to‘liq qayta yig‘iladi deb taxmin qilinmaydi. AMT PC’dan tashqarida nusxa yo‘q: lokal snapshot faqat mantiqiy xatodan (yomon migration, o‘chirish, noto‘g‘ri ommaviy yozuv) himoya qiladi. Ikkala diskning yo‘qolishi yoki o‘g‘irlanishi — qabul qilingan host-loss doirasi (Q55): AMT tarixi (approval, audit, readiness imzolari, ledger’lar) yo‘qoladi; lead’lar landing store va amoCRM’da qoladi, CAPI hodisalari ulardan Meta’ning event yoshi oynasida qayta yuboriladi, lekin faqat yo‘qolgan host yubora olmaganlari — takroriy server eventi dedup’iga tayanilmaydi (qoida `operations.yaml#host_loss.rebuild_from_sources`); qolganlari `skipped_unknown_prior_send` bilan audit qilinadi; o‘g‘irlik — INC-26.

Backup stale alert — jadvaldan olingan run deadline’i (02:00 + kutilgan run davomiyligi + margin) o‘tsa darhol alert va bitta catch-up run; shu payt ma’lumot yo‘qotish oynasi 24 soatdan oshishi mumkin va ko‘rsatiladi (`operations.yaml#backup_stale_alert_rule`).

Restore **har doim `killed` bilan boshlanadi** (◇ P-16); `shadow`+L0 ga faqat reconcile tugagandan keyin owner qadami bilan o‘tiladi. Ajratilgan credential, job va remote write egress yo‘q. Qaytish tartibi: read/ingest → remote reconciliation → consent-valid CAPI backfill → tracking/freshness gate → owner resume. Resume darajasi — `min(snapshot_mode, off-host control journal’dagi oxirgi rejim, L1_approve_each)`; journal bo‘lmasa barcha pipeline L0 va owner har birini alohida ochadi. Kill, rejim o‘zgarishi, rol bekor qilinishi va resume off-host journal’ga ko‘zgulanadi. Eski approval, action yoki yangi aktivlik avtomatik tirilmaydi. Barcha fault testlar isolated; haqiqiy remote integratsiya owner tasdiqlagan PAUSED entity va simulyatsiya qilingan activation bilan — production’da global `shadow` rejimida, F3-AT-05 dagi `origin=test` istisnosi bo‘yicha (`staging` va `restore_drill` da emas).

Maintenance hold kill emas: `{global_mode: read_only, proxy_admission: paused, restore_previous_modes_on_release: true}`; L2 pasaytirilmaydi; oyna tugagach `maintenance_release` bilan faqat hold o‘zgartirgan o‘lchovlar (global rejim, proxy admission) tiklanadi, hold paytida yoqilgan kill va himoya nazoratlari o‘z chiqish qoidasigacha qoladi (`controls_raised_during_hold_persist`). Upgrade oynasi — 14:00–18:00, dushanba emas (★, `operations.yaml#upgrade_window`). Incident va rollback esa kill/resume qoidalari bilan ishlaydi.

## 7. Release, readiness va kuzatuv

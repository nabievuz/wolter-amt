# Production qabul mezonlari va dalillar

Holat: spetsifikatsiya · Runtime: NOT_RUN · Sana: 2026-10-23.

Quyidagi testlar Wolter AMT production tizimi uchun bajarilishi kerak. **Joriy holat: barcha testlar `not_run`**. Bu fayl majburiy test spetsifikatsiyasi; yashil test yoki runtime tayyorligi da’vosi emas. Kanonik talablar [INVARIANTS](../11_Production_Contracts/INVARIANTS.md), qiymat va gate yopilishi [CONFIG_CONTRACT](../11_Production_Contracts/CONFIG_CONTRACT.md) va [readiness.yaml](../09_Config_Templates/readiness.yaml) orqali. F0–F7 va FE faza acceptance testlari ([PHASES](../05_Build_Phases/PHASES.md)) saqlanadi; UAT-01…UAT-34 biznes va ekspluatatsiya qabulini, EVO-AT01…EVO-AT14 workflow va self-evolution qabulini, [WORKFLOW_TESTS](WORKFLOW_TESTS.md) esa har workflow’ning end-to-end va xato yo‘llari testlarini (WF-TEST-*) beradi.

## Test muhitining sharti

Fault va load sinovlari avval mock/replay yoki ajratilgan test host/DB/queue’da bajariladi. Muhit resurslari [environments.yaml](../09_Config_Templates/environments.yaml) dan olinadi; staging, sandbox va restore nusxasi prod bilan hech bir resursda kesishmaydi (validator tekshiradi). `staging` va `restore_drill` — AMT PC’dagi o‘z systemd-nspawn konteyneri va network namespace’i (ichida Docker yo‘q; servislar oddiy jarayon sifatida), o‘z control socket va kill latch yo‘llari, o‘z PostgreSQL klasteri va `sandbox` slice; staging egress’i faqat uning veth’i uchun `DOCKER-USER` zanjiridagi allowlist orqali (Docker `FORWARD` siyosatini `DROP` qiladi; `restore_drill` da tarmoq yo‘q), uning credential’lari faqat test uchun (staging Telegram bot, CAPI test event kodi) va hech qachon production reklama akkauntiga yozish credential’i emas. Staging va restore nusxalarida production job/routine, webhook va live credential’lar o‘chiq; tashqi yozish default-deny; restore start `killed`. Nazoratli haqiqiy integratsiya faqat ruxsatlangan PAUSED `origin=test` Meta obyektlari va oldindan belgilangan test resurslarida bajariladi; activation simulyatsiya qilinadi. Kill dry run’dagi owner tasdiqlagan PAUSED Meta test integratsiyasi (F6-AT-08 (2)) production’da global `shadow` rejimida, F3-AT-05 dagi `origin=test` PAUSED-fixture istisnosi bo‘yicha (production credential’lari, faqat PAUSED test entity, activation simulyatsiya; faqat B kill ishlatadigan pauza chaqiruvi `origin=test` item orqali sinaladi, production kill bajarilmaydi — production `control_epoch` va kill latch o‘zgarmaydi) Step 1 dan oldin bajariladi — hech qachon `staging` yoki `restore_drill` da emas. Bu hujjat jonli sarf yoki tashqi xabar yuborishga ruxsat bermaydi. Restore testi CRM/CAPI/Telegram production yozishlarini ham chiqarmasligi isbotlanadi.

## Dalil yozuvi

Har test natijasi: `test_id`, `run_at_utc`, muhit/host, `release_id` va manifest digest (code, workflow definition, config, schema, model, evaluator revision’lari), `workflow_definition_digest`, `evaluator_revision`, `run_id`/`step_run_id`/`command_id` (bo‘lsa), input fixture yoki redacted real sample, tekshirish qadami, kutilgan va kuzatilgan natija, natija holati, log/query/artifact havolasi, bajaruvchi va mustaqil ko‘rib chiqqan shaxs. Tashqi servis ishlagan testda provider request ID yoki moslashtirilgan entity/event ID bo‘ladi; token va PII chiqarilmaydi.

**Natija lug‘ati (yagona):** `not_run | pass | fail | blocked | inconclusive` — data’da kichik harf; katta harf faqat ko‘rinishda.

| Natija | Ma’nosi |
|---|---|
| `not_run` | bajarilmagan |
| `pass` | kutilgan natija oldindan tasdiqlangan chegara ichida, dalil bilan |
| `fail` | kutilgan natija yo‘q yoki chegara buzilgan |
| `blocked` | sharti yo‘q: readiness `pending`, chegara tasdiqlanmagan, muhit yoki dalil yetishmaydi. PASS emas |
| `inconclusive` | faqat biznes/statistik testlarda (sample, maturity yoki taqqoslash yetarli emas). Gate, rollout yoki Trust Ramp uchun PASS hisoblanmaydi (◇ P-04) |

Sonli chegara zarur bo‘lsa u testdan oldin tasdiqlangan policy yoki readiness qiymatida bo‘ladi (masalan `ops.kill_targets`, `ops.resource_envelope`, `recovery.*`). Chegarasiz “tez ishladi”, o‘lchanmagan “restore bo‘ldi” yoki manbasiz “foyda oshdi” PASS emas. Sintetik fixture’dagi pul va sonlar haqiqiy biznes natijasi sifatida hisobotga kirmaydi.

## Talab bilan bog‘lanish

Har UAT, EVO-AT, WF-TEST va faza AT qaysi talabni isbotlashi [REQUIREMENT_TRACEABILITY.json](REQUIREMENT_TRACEABILITY.json) da talab darajasida yuritiladi: `requirements[]` (FR-*, NFR-*, EVO-R01…EVO-R15 — `source_q`, `canonical`, `tests`), `invariants` (I01…I25 → testlar) va `phase_acceptance_ids`. Bu jadvallardagi har test ID’si o‘sha faylda kamida bitta talab yoki invariantga, yoki `requirement_refs` bilan talabga bog‘langan workflow definition’iga bog‘lanadi (validator Q07); PRD va INVARIANTS’dagi test ustunlari shu fayl bilan bir xil (Q08); u yerda tilga olingan har test ID shu hujjatda, [WORKFLOW_TESTS](WORKFLOW_TESTS.md) yoki faza fayllarida mavjud. Nomuvofiqlik, noma’lum ID yoki talabsiz test — validator xatosi.

## Yakuniy gate tartibi

Gate’lar [readiness.yaml](../09_Config_Templates/readiness.yaml) `profiles` DAG’iga mos. Profil PASS = o‘zi va meros olgan barcha profillarning har talabi `approved` va `max_evidence_age`dan eski emas, yoki to‘g‘ri `not_applicable` (faqat `criticality: optional`, sabab va owner imzosi bilan); critical talab N/A bo‘lmaydi. Runtime capability faqat o‘z gate’idan o‘tganda ochiladi — release’da yoqiladigan capability release snapshot’ida, owner o‘tishi bilan ochiladigani `control_events`da — va gate’lar orasida `operational_validity` bilan ishlaydi (`execution-policy.yaml` `capability_requires_profile`; `readiness.yaml#rules`).

1. **F1 profillari (F1_AUTH, F1_COST_RUNTIME, F1_ISOLATION, F1_WORKFLOW):** owner identity va break-glass, billable LLM faqat proxy orqali, pinned komponentlar va runner prototipi, workflow runtime prototipi (WF-TEST-RT-*).
2. **F2 real trafik oldidan (F2_LIVE_INTAKE; HG-F2-14, HG-F2-15, HG-F2-16, HG-F2-17, HG-F2-18):** intake durability, consent, production landing qaytarish yo‘li, `recovery.intake_datasets` va `recovery.intake_drill`, `ops.intake_responders`, mustaqil intake monitoringi va alternate channel, `ops.credential_inventory`, `privacy.breach_response`, landing uchun `offers.initial` va `offers.asset_rights`.
3. **Tracking PASS (F2_TRACKING_PASS; HG-F2-08):** 14 kun report-only, tasdiqlangan chegaralar, 7 ketma-ket PASS.
4. **F3_CONTROLLED_TEST:** `vendor.meta_cap` read-back, `vendor.meta_access_tier`, `ops.kill_targets`, read tool allowlist.
5. **F4_AGENTS va F4_CONTENT:** olti agent haqiqiy rol izolyatsiyasi bilan, barcha pipeline L0; offer, asset huquqi va media chegaralari.
6. **FE_SANDBOX va FE_RELEASE:** FE himoyalangan release yo‘li `verified_in_staging`, deployed va FE_RELEASE PASS — F5 formal eval va shadow boshlanishidan oldin, Q66 exact baseline mustaqil texnik inson signoff’i bilan (Q67; OD-01 yopildi).
7. **F5 GO (F5_GO; HG-F5-02):** ikki alohida savol. **A) Operatsion xavfsizlik:** GO/NO-GO dalil bilan (konfiguratsiya yopilishi, eval/shadow xavfsizligi, xarajat, UAT-33/34, readiness). **B) Biznes asosi:** `sufficient | inconclusive | insufficient` va exposure qarori. B `sufficient` bo‘lmasa F6 faqat rent L1 va tasdiqlangan exposure ichida; Step 2 hech qachon avtomatik emas. Kelajakdagi live biznes foydasi shadow natijasi bilan almashtirilmaydi.
8. **F6 Step 1 oldidan (F6_STEP1; HG-F6-03, HG-F6-04 rent, HG-F6-06, keyin HG-F6-01):** kill, restore, rollback, pul nazorati, ijro va inson davomiyligi testlari bajarilgan. F6-AT-13 tekshiradi: HG-F6-03, HG-F6-04 (rent), HG-F6-06, F6_STEP1 profili PASS, ads_approver grant’i (muddati Step 1 + 28 kun) va F6-AT-01/03/03b/04/08/09/10/11/12/14 vaqtlari birinchi live dispatch’dan oldin.
9. **F6 Step 2 oldidan (F6_STEP2; HG-F6-02):** rent bosqichi dalili (`business.step1_comparison`, KC-01 ning ikkinchi baholashi bajarilgan — ◇ P-13), `sale.readiness`, `ops.sale_handback_roster`; yetarli bo‘lmasa `inconclusive`, boshqaruv avtomatik kengaymaydi.
10. **L2_ENABLE (HG-L2-01, har action_type × pipeline):** ketma-ket 10 ta `met` natija + 14 kun insidentsiz ★, izolyatsiya dalili, owner `/graduate`.
11. **AUTO_RELEASE_ENABLE:** o‘chiq; yoqish Q11 ni o‘zgartiruvchi yangi owner Q (⧗ OD-03) va `auto_release.decision_ref`ni talab qiladi.
12. **To‘liq qabul:** barcha F0–F7 va FE faza AT’lari (F7-AT-06 biznes qabul va F7-AT-07 davomiy ops bilan), UAT-01…UAT-34, EVO-AT01…EVO-AT14 va barcha WF-TEST-* natijalari, kalibrlash, runbook egasi hamda real biznes kuzatuvi dalili mavjud.
